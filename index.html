07 FAQ

Your questions.
My answers.

How does the 10-day design & video sprint work?

After you pick a plan or book a call, you fill a 5-minute brief and upload your footage or brand references. On Day 4 you review the first concept or rough cut, we refine together on Day 7, and on Day 10 you get the final 4K masters and source files.

Who owns the source files (PSD, AI, Figma, Premiere, After Effects)?

You own 100% of the work made for your brand. Every delivery includes clean, organised source files plus full commercial licensing.

Can I combine graphic design and video editing in one plan?

Yes — the Full Creative Partner plan combines brand design, high-CTR thumbnails, social banners and long/short-form video editing in one flat monthly price.

Can I cancel or pause my monthly plan anytime?

Absolutely. No long-term lock-in. Quiet month? Pause or cancel, effective at the end of your current billing period.
